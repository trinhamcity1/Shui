A starting opinion

The Current Underline is the one I'd bet on first — it's the most literal answer to "everything in one drawing," it survives every background in the check row above without a legibility fight, and the flowing stroke can be reused on its own elsewhere (loading states, a section divider) once it's established here.

The Dotted Current is the quiet, safe alternative if the instinct is to change as little about "Shui" as possible — real type, one custom detail, done. The Ripple Seal and The Poured Word are the two with the most personality and the most specific use cases (a seal, a badge) rather than being the everyday wordmark. The Threaded Current is the boldest of the five and the one most worth testing at real app-icon size before trusting it — weaving behind the letters is a strong idea that gets harder to read the smaller it gets.
